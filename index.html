National Declaration

A Brotherly Model of Regional Sovereignty

The Republic of Crete (TRC) is a symbolic, devolved model federation celebrating the deep heritage, wild independence, and agricultural pride of Greece's largest island.

Built as an artistic parallel simulation, the TRC honors the eternal bonds with mainland Greece while mapping out an imaginary, high-tech autonomous framework across our four historic federal states: Chania, Rethymno, Heraklion, and Lasithi.
